WHAT WE MAKE
Heatloop is a thermal battery for homes. It charges from cheap off-peak
electricity, stores the energy as heat in sealed phase-change cells that
melt at 58 C, and delivers it to wet radiators and hot taps on demand.
No combustion, no flue, no gas supply.
