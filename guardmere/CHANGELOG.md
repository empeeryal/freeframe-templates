# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.4] - 2026-11-19

### Fixed

- After you go to another page and press Back, the search boxes and filters on the services, readiness, case
  studies and field notes pages and the scope on the assessment page go back to what the page shows, and the
  call fields on the contact page follow the box the browser puts back.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-07

### Changed

- American wording throughout, including US terms for checkboxes, business days, schedules and units.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- Set in Huntsville, Alabama, with American spelling, US dollars, US phone numbers and local details.
- The consultation calendar now closes on US federal holidays, worked out by rule for any year, and uses Central time with a 12-hour clock.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The guide, README and changelog use American spelling. The template itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Nine HTML pages for an independent security practice with twelve individually illustrated review areas.
- A saved assessment calculator, evidence-readiness checklist, engagement library and searchable field notes.
- Consultation requests with rolling working days, inline validation and clear next steps.
- Original artwork, Google Fonts, responsive navigation and complete documentation.
