## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Nine complete HTML pages for modular educational robotics, with sixteen individually illustrated modules.
- A compatibility, power and cost kit configurator with named builds and module comparisons.
- Six practical projects, twelve field notes, three team profiles and validated enquiry success states.
- A kit quote request that carries the kit bench parts list, number of kits and total into the enquiry form and
  returns a quote reference with next steps.
- An ages 11+ and small-parts note wherever kits and modules are described.
- Original SVG artwork, Google Fonts typography and full customisation documentation.
