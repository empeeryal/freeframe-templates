# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-23

### Fixed

- The fitting request and enquiry forms no longer finish by preparing an email, saying the request "has not been
  sent" or that "nothing is sent from this page". The fitting request now confirms the request was received, shows
  a summary and a reference like PL-7K2Q, and promises an email within one working day to confirm the time. The
  enquiry form confirms the enquiry with a reply within two working days and a reference. `DOCS.md` and
  `template.json` describe the new behaviour.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-19

### Fixed

- The fitting calendar's day buttons and its Earlier and Later buttons now have accessible names that start with their visible text (WCAG 2.5.3).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added

- Ten complete pages for an independent alterations and repair studio.
- Garment-specific services, a multi-garment estimate and saved repair plan.
- A rolling fitting calendar with specialist availability, England and Wales bank holidays and request review.
- A filterable work ledger, searchable care notes and email enquiry preparation.
- Original drawings for each garment, case study and specialist, an icon for every service, and full documentation.
