## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-19

### Fixed

- After you go to another page and press Back, the search boxes and filters on the workspace, integrations and
  resources pages go back to what the page shows, and the walkthrough fields on the contact page follow the
  topic the browser puts back.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- Set in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, with American spelling, US dollars, US phone numbers and local details.
- The walkthrough calendar now skips US federal holidays, worked out by rule for any year, and uses Eastern time.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-05

### Changed

- The guide, README and changelog use American spelling. The template itself is unchanged.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Nine complete HTML pages for the Planwick product-planning service.
- A sixteen-initiative roadmap, editable priority scoring, release scope and rolling capacity plan.
- Seat pricing, integration rollout choices, team approaches and a searchable planning library.
- A roadmap product mock on the home page, customer wordmarks and numbers, quotes, three customer stories and a
  security and trust section.
- Original SVG artwork, Google Fonts, accessible interactions and complete documentation.
