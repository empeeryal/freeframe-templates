From provisional to pass

Four junctions between you and a full licence.

  1. 1

    Provisional licence

    Apply from 15 years and 9 months. You can drive with us from your 17th birthday.

  2. 2

    First lesson

    Two hours, £25 an hour for new pupils. Your instructor works out a plan and a rough number of hours.

  3. 3

    Theory test

    Practise with our road-sense quiz, then book it while you keep driving.

  4. 4

    Practical test

    When your instructor says you’re ready, in the car you learned in, with a warm-up lesson first.
