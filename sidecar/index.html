01 / What we do

One small team for design, code and the launch.

Founders hire us when the product has to exist before the next raise, and there is no time to hire a whole team first. You get senior people who have done this 38 times, working in your Slack, your repo and your calendar.

Service 01

Product design

Research with your users, flows, prototypes tested every week and a design system your next hire can pick up.

Usually 4 to 8 weeks

Service 02

Engineering

Web and mobile apps, APIs and the integrations that make them useful, built to be grown by your own team.

Usually 8 to 16 weeks

Service 03

Launch

Positioning, the launch page, onboarding and the first 30 days of numbers, planned with you week by week.

Usually 3 to 6 weeks
