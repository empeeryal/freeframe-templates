## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added

- Ten HTML pages for Sidecar, a product design and engineering studio for seed-stage startups in Little Rock, Arkansas.
- Work index with eight projects, filters by service and funding stage and a grid or list layout, all kept in the URL so a filtered view can be linked.
- Full Brineworks case study with project facts, a sketch-to-shipped compare slider, a 14-week timeline, results and a founder quote.
- Engagement estimator: pick services, team size and weeks, switch between a fixed sprint and a monthly retainer, and see a price range, a sample timeline and dates, saved in the browser and shareable by URL.
- Services page with an engagement model helper, a 12-week process with keyboard-accessible week tabs and a founder hours chart, and a team page with drawn portraits, a group filter and a live studio clock.
- Careers page with discipline and location filters and expanding role details, and a five-step project brief with validation, estimator prefill and next open start dates that roll forward from today.
- Drawn artwork throughout, a social image and full documentation.
