About · Since 2019

Fourteen people on South Main who like the messy first version.

Ottavia Varner and Delmar Haskins started Sidecar after building three startups’ first products from a back table at a Little Rock coffee shop. The idea was simple: ride alongside founders for one quarter, then get out of the way.

What we believe

Four rules on the wall above the workbench.

  1. 1.

    Ship by Friday

    Something real, every week. A slide is not a demo.

  2. 2.

    Sit where it hurts

    Barns, kitchens, truck cabs, clinic back offices. We go to the user.

  3. 3.

    Say the hard thing early

    If a feature should not exist, you will hear it in week two, not week ten.

  4. 4.

    Leave it better owned

    The goal is a founder who no longer needs us. Most still call.
