Process · 12 weeks

A typical engagement, one week at a time.

Every project bends this plan, but the bones stay: frame it, shape it, build it with real users watching, launch it and hand over every key.

Week by week

Frame Shape Build Harden Launch
Frame Week 1 of 12

Kickoff and the problem on one page

Two days in Little Rock or at your office. We interview you, read everything you have and talk to five of your users or would-be users.

Who is on it

    You get

    A one-page problem statement both sides sign

    Founder time this week

    6 hours

    Founder time, all 12 weeks

    Hours a week we need from someone who can make product calls. Select a bar to open that week.

    39 hours in total

    Every week, on the same days

    • MonPlan the week together, 30 minutes
    • WedCheck-in in Slack, written
    • FriDemo of working software, recorded

    After week 12

    Handoff means you could fire us the next day.

    (Please don’t. But you could.)

    1. A

      Every account in your name

      Repository, hosting, app stores, analytics, domain and email. We remove ourselves on the last day.

    2. B

      Docs a new engineer can follow

      Setup in one command, architecture notes and a recorded walkthrough of each part of the system.

    3. C

      30 days of support

      A shared channel stays open. Bugs we wrote, we fix, free.

    4. D

      A plan for the next 90 days

      What to build next, who to hire first and what to measure, written with you.
