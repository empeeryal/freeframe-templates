    How we hire

    Four steps, about three weeks, no take-home homework.

    1. Step 1 · 30 min

      Intro call

      With Thatcher, about you, the role and the pay range.

    2. Step 2 · 60 min

      Show us your work

      Walk us through something you shipped, and what you would change.

    3. Step 3 · 2 hours, paid

      A morning with us

      Pair on a real problem with two of the team. We pay you $300 for it.

    4. Step 4 · 45 min

      Partner chat

      With Ottavia or Delmar. Then a decision within three working days.
