Billing questions

Something else? Call us at (859) 555-0147.

Who counts as an employee?

Full-time and part-time employees on your payroll on the first day of each billing period. Contractors and seasonal workers employed for less than 90 days don’t count.

What happens when we grow?

We true up once a quarter, not every month. If you grow by more than 10% in a quarter, the extra employees are billed for the rest of your term at your current rate.

Is there a minimum?

Plans start at 50 employees. A 30-person company pays the 50-employee price, which is $200 a month on Essentials with yearly billing.

Do you charge for implementation?

No. Every plan includes setup help, data migration from your current system or spreadsheets, and training for your hiring managers. Most customers post their first job within two weeks.

Can we pay by invoice?

Yes. Yearly plans can pay by card, ACH or invoice with net-30 terms. Monthly plans pay by card or ACH.

What if we cancel?

Monthly plans stop at the end of the month. Yearly plans run to the end of the term. Either way, you can export every candidate, scorecard and report as CSV and PDF for 90 days.
