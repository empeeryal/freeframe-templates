Security and compliance

Candidate data is personal. We treat it that way.

Résumés, interview notes and self-ID answers are some of the most sensitive records a company keeps. Here is how we protect them, how long we keep them and who can see them.

Request our security packet Data retention

Controls we run, and test every year.

Slatework completes a SOC 2 Type II examination by an independent CPA firm each year. Customers on any plan can read the latest report under a nondisclosure agreement.

  • Access control

    Role-based permissions per job, so an interviewer sees only the candidates they meet. Single sign-on and automatic deprovisioning on Enterprise.

  • Encryption

    TLS 1.2 or later in transit and AES-256 at rest, including backups and résumé files. Keys rotate yearly.

  • Audit logging

    Every profile view, export, stage change and permission change is logged with who and when, kept for two years and searchable by admins.

  • Hosting and resilience

    Hosted in two US regions with hourly backups, a four-hour recovery objective and a restore test every quarter.

  • Vendor management

    Every subprocessor is reviewed before onboarding and every year after. We give 30 days’ notice before adding one.

  • People and change

    Background-checked employees, yearly security training, peer-reviewed code changes and an annual third-party penetration test.

Keep candidate data only as long as you need it.

Set how long Slatework keeps candidates after their last activity. When the period ends, we anonymize the profile: names, contact details, résumés and notes are removed, and the counts in your reports stay.

Candidates can ask to see or delete their data at any time from the link in every email we send for you.

Voluntary self-ID, kept apart from hiring decisions.

Employers with federal reporting duties, including many federal contractors, may need applicant demographic data. Slatework collects it on a separate, clearly voluntary step and stores it apart from the candidate profile.

  • Separate storage and access

    Self-ID answers never appear on profiles, scorecards or exports used by interviewers. Only roles you grant reporting access can see summaries.

  • Summary reports by job and stage

    Applicant flow by job group and stage, ready to support EEO-1 and affirmative action planning, with small groups suppressed in on-screen views.

  • Disposition reasons on every rejection

    Required, job-related reasons when a candidate is rejected, so your records explain each decision.

This page is general information about how Slatework works, not legal advice. Your obligations depend on your size, contracts and locations; confirm them with your own counsel.
