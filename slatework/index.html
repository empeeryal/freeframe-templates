            One system, start to signed

            Everything between “we need someone” and their first day.

            Slatework replaces the job board logins, the interview spreadsheet and the offer letter in someone’s downloads folder. Each step hands off to the next, so nothing waits on a status email.

            • Post everywhere at once

              Write the posting once, pick your boards, and every application lands in the same pipeline with its source tagged.

              Sourcing and job boards

            • One shared pipeline

              Recruiters, hiring managers and interviewers work from the same board, with days-in-stage flags so nobody sits in Screen for three weeks.

              Pipeline and stages

            • Structured interviews

              Weighted scorecards and interview kits for every role. The recommendation comes from the ratings, before anyone hears the loudest opinion.

              Scorecards and kits

            • Scheduling across time zones

              Slatework reads every interviewer’s calendar and proposes times that work for the whole panel, shown in the candidate’s own time zone.

              Interview scheduling

            • Offers with approvals

              Offer letters from templates, checked against the pay band, approved by the right people and signed online.

              Offers and approvals

            • Reports leadership trusts

              Time to hire, source of hire, pass-through rates and voluntary self-ID summaries, ready for your Monday meeting.

              Hiring reports

            Structured interviews

            Five competencies. Four ratings. One clear recommendation.

            Every interviewer scores the same competencies on the same scale, weighted by what matters for the role. Change a rating on the scorecard and watch the recommendation follow.

            • Scorecards are due within 24 hours, with reminders in chat
            • Interviewers can’t see each other’s ratings until they submit
            • Job-related questions only, from a kit you write once per role
