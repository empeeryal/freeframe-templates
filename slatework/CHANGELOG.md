## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added

- Nine HTML pages for Slatework, an applicant tracking system for growing companies in Lexington, Kentucky.
- Live hero pipeline board: drag candidates between Applied, Screen, Interview, Offer and Hired with a mouse or by touch, or move them from a keyboard-accessible menu, with stage counts, a funnel and projected time to hire updating live and saved in the browser.
- Product tour with a job board picker and posting preview, a weighted interview scorecard, a panel scheduler that proposes times across US time zones and an offer pay band check with approval routing.
- Per-employee pricing with a monthly or yearly switch, a cost and cost-per-hire calculator with shareable URLs, a comparison table and a billing FAQ.
- Customer stories filtered by industry and company size, each with its own logo mark and before-and-after chart.
- Searchable integrations directory with category filters, shareable URLs, a drawn mark for each of 22 integrations and a detail dialog.
- Security and compliance page with a data retention example, resources with articles and rolling monthly webinars, a demo request form that proposes a meeting time, light and dark themes and full documentation.
